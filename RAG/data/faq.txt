Employee FAQs – NexaTech Solutions

1. Q: How many leaves do I get?
   A: 12 casual, 10 sick, and 18 earned leaves annually.

2. Q: What are working hours?
   A: 9 AM to 6 PM with flexible start time.

3. Q: Can I work remotely?
   A: Yes, up to 3 days per week.

4. Q: How do I apply for leave?
   A: Through the internal HR portal.

5. Q: When is salary credited?
   A: Last working day of the month.

6. Q: What is the notice period?
   A: 30 days.

7. Q: Who do I contact for IT issues?
   A: IT Support Team via email.

8. Q: Is there a dress code?
   A: Smart casuals.

9. Q: Are public holidays included?
   A: Yes, as per company calendar.

10. Q: Can unused leaves be carried forward?
    A: Yes, earned leave up to 30 days.

11. Q: Is overtime paid?
    A: Depends on project and manager approval.

12. Q: What tools are used for communication?
    A: Slack and Email.

13. Q: Is training provided?
    A: Yes, regular upskilling sessions.

14. Q: Can I switch teams internally?
    A: Yes, based on performance and availability.

15. Q: What is core working time?
    A: 11 AM to 4 PM.

16. Q: Is work-from-home permanent?
    A: Only for approved roles.

17. Q: Are laptops provided?
    A: Yes, company-issued devices.

18. Q: What happens during emergencies?
    A: Inform manager immediately.

19. Q: Is health insurance provided?
    A: Yes, for all employees.

20. Q: How is performance evaluated?
    A: Quarterly reviews.

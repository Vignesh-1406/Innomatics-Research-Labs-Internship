 HR Policy Document

Company Name: NexaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

 1. Introduction

This HR policy outlines the guidelines, rules, and benefits applicable to all employees of NexaTech Solutions. It ensures a fair, transparent, and productive work environment.


 2. Working Hours

 Standard working hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
 Total working hours: 40 hours per week
 Flexible timing allowed between 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM start
 Employees must complete 8 hours/day


 3. Leave Policy

 Types of Leave:

 Casual Leave (CL): 12 days/year
 Sick Leave (SL): 10 days/year
 Earned Leave (EL): 18 days/year
 Public Holidays: As per company calendar

 Rules:

 Leaves must be applied via internal portal
 Sick leave beyond 2 days requires medical proof
 Unused EL can be carried forward (max 30 days)


 4. Remote Work Policy

 Employees may work remotely up to 3 days/week
 Full remote allowed for approved roles
 Must be available during core hours: 11 AM – 4 PM
 Stable internet and productivity tools required


 5. Code of Conduct

 Maintain professionalism and respect
 No harassment or discrimination
 Protect company data and confidentiality


 6. Payroll

 Salary is credited on last working day of the month
 Payslips available via employee portal


 7. IT & Security Policy

 Use company systems responsibly
 Do not share passwords or confidential data
 Report security incidents immediately


 8. Policy Updates

Company reserves the right to modify policies as needed.
